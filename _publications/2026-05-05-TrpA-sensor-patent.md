## Abstract
The present invention provides a tryptamine-responsive fluorescent probe, along with its preparation method and applications. Specifically, the invention discloses a tryptamine-binding protein and a tryptamine-responsive probe designed for dynamic and reversible detection of tryptamine. The probe can be used for quantitative detection of tryptamine in vitro, at subcellular or in situ levels, and in vivo, offering high spatiotemporal resolution, rapid kinetic response, high specificity, and high sensitivity. It shows no or weak response to other neuromodulators such as indole and serotonin, thereby enabling specific recognition of trace amounts of tryptamine in complex biological systems, even against high concentrations of tryptophan and structurally similar serotonin. This allows precise monitoring of dynamic changes in tryptamine concentration, which is of great significance for elucidating its physiological functions and the mechanisms of related diseases.
